Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Who this covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Darrin Nguyen, operator of the automated SMS platform "Tennis Rules Text Line," handles information collected through that platform.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used to send automated SMS replies back to you.
  • Message content — the text of each rules question you send, used to generate the matching answer.

No other information — such as location, contacts, or device data — is collected through this platform.

How we use your information

Your phone number and message content are used solely by the automated platform to generate and deliver an SMS answer to the specific question you sent, and for basic troubleshooting of the service itself.

Information sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Messages are retained only as long as needed to operate and troubleshoot the platform, and are not used for any purpose beyond answering the question sent.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out — no further messages will be sent.
  • Reply HELP for program information and support contact details.
Questions about this policy can be sent to darrinnguyen2@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Tennis Rules Text Line SMS platform operated by Darrin Nguyen and may be updated from time to time.
